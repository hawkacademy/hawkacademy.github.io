How to prepare

From a single task number to a whole exam — in the order confidence grows.

  1. Pick a task number

    Every time a new task of the same type as on the exam, with different numbers or options.

  2. Check your answer

    The answer is checked at once. Then a step-by-step solution, plus a 📏 note when the answer format has a catch.

  3. Open the cheat sheet

    For every task number: how to solve it, tips, common mistakes and formulas. Opens right from the task.

  4. Sit a mock OGE

    A full exam with a timer. At the end — points, a grade on the official scale and the history of all attempts.

Written answers, graded by FIPI criteria

A summary, an essay or a part 2 solution can’t be checked fairly by a machine. So after you answer, the app shows a model answer and the official FIPI criteria, and you score yourself — the way an examiner will.

You see where points get lost before the exam. After self-checking a summary or an essay, words with possible spelling mistakes are highlighted as a hint for the literacy criterion.

Frequently asked questions

What is the OGE?

The OGE (Basic State Exam) is the exam students in Russia take at the end of 9th grade. Task formats and grading rules are set by FIPI, the Federal Institute of Pedagogical Measurements.

Which subjects does the app cover?

Maths (25 tasks), Russian language (13 tasks), geography (30 tasks) and computer science (16 tasks). Social studies and biology are marked as coming soon in the app.

What language is the app in?

Russian, like the exam itself.

Do I need an internet connection or an account?

No. The app works offline and without an account. Statistics, essay drafts and exam history are stored only on the phone.

Which devices are supported?

iPhone with iOS 17 or later.
