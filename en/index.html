How it works

From one task number to a whole exam

In the order confidence grows.

  1. Pick a task number

    Every time a new task of the same type as on the exam, with different numbers, words or options.

  2. Check your answer

    The answer is checked at once. Then a step-by-step solution, plus a 📏 note when the answer format has a catch.

  3. Open the cheat sheet

    For every task number: how to solve it, tips, common mistakes, formulas and reference data.

  4. Sit a mock OGE

    A full exam with a timer. At the end — points, a grade on the official scale and the history of all attempts.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

What is the OGE?

The OGE (Basic State Exam) is the exam students in Russia take at the end of 9th grade. Task formats and grading rules are set by FIPI, the Federal Institute of Pedagogical Measurements.

Which subjects does the app cover?

Seven: maths (25 tasks), Russian language (13), English (38, including the oral part), geography (30), computer science (16), social studies (20, the 2027 model) and biology (26).

What language is the app in?

Russian, like the exam itself. The English course is in English, with explanations in Russian.

Can a tutor work with a student in the app?

Yes. Start a FaceTime call and tap SharePlay in the app: both phones show the same task, the same cheat sheet or the same mock exam, there is a shared whiteboard, and written answers are graded together by the criteria. Three or more people can join a group call.

Do I need an internet connection or an account?

No. The app works offline and without an account. Statistics, essay drafts, voice recordings and exam history are stored only on the phone. SharePlay needs a FaceTime call, as any call does.

Which devices are supported?

iPhone with iOS 17 or later. The whiteboard also works on iPad.

A question, an idea, or found a mistake in a task?

Write to us — we will reply and fix it. Schools, tutors and partners are welcome too.
