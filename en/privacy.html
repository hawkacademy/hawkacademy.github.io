Privacy Policy

Hawk Academy: OGE 2027 app for iPhone. Effective 22 October 2026.

In short: the app does not collect, share or sell your data. It works offline and without an account, and everything you do in it is stored only on your iPhone.

Who we are

The Hawk Academy: OGE 2027 app (the “app”) is developed and published by Hawk Academy. For any question about data, write to hawk.academy.education@gmail.com.

What the app stores, and where

To show your progress and keep your work, the app saves the following in its own storage on the device:

  • answer statistics for each task number and flash-card learning progress;
  • the history and archive of mock exams, including an exam still in progress;
  • your summaries and essays, drafts and your self-assessment scores;
  • your voice recordings from the English oral part, until you delete them;
  • settings, such as the programming language chosen for computer science.

This data never leaves the phone: the app has no server and sends it nowhere. If iCloud or computer backup is turned on, the app’s data may be included in the backup — you control this in iOS settings, and it is governed by Apple’s privacy policy.

What the app does not do

  • It does not ask you to sign up and never asks for your name, email, phone number, age or school.
  • It does not request access to location, contacts or the camera. The microphone is used only for the English oral part: recording starts when you tap the button, and the recording stays on the phone. Photo access is used only by the “Save to Photos” button on the draft sheet, which saves your own drawing to an album.
  • It contains no advertising, no analytics and no third-party SDKs that collect data.
  • It does not track you across other apps or websites and does not use the advertising identifier.
  • It does not share or sell data to third parties.

Built-in iOS features

Highlighting of possible spelling mistakes in summaries and essays uses the built-in iOS spell checker; texts are read aloud from recordings inside the app or by the built-in iOS voice. Both work on the device and need no internet.

Children

The app is made for 9th-grade students. We collect no personal data from children or adults.

How to delete data

  • Statistics and exam history for a subject: the subject’s statistics screen → “Сбросить статистику по предмету” (Reset subject statistics).
  • All data at once: delete the app from your iPhone — everything it stored is deleted with it.

If you write to us

When you email support, we receive your email address and your message. We use them only to reply and fix the problem, never share them, and delete the conversation on request.

This website

The site uses no cookies, counters or third-party fonts. The page language is chosen from your device’s time zone: Russian in Russia, English elsewhere. This happens in your browser and nothing is sent anywhere. If you switch the language manually, the choice is remembered in your browser’s storage (localStorage) on this device.

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages: when pages are opened, GitHub may log technical data such as IP addresses to protect against attacks — see the GitHub General Privacy Statement.

Changes

If the app ever starts handling data differently, we will update this page before that version is released and change the date at the top.
