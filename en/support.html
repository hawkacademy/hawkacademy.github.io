Found a mistake in a task, something isn’t working, or have an idea? Write to us in English or Russian — we will reply and fix it.

Email hawk.academy.education@gmail.com

Reporting a mistake in a task

  • The subject and task number, for example “Maths, task 15”.
  • A screenshot of the task and its solution, so we can find that exact task.
  • Which answer you think is correct, and why.

If something is broken, add your iPhone model and iOS version (Settings → General → About).

Common questions

My answer is right, but the app didn’t accept it

Check the format: on the OGE, answers are written without spaces, without commas between digits and without units, and a common fraction is written as a decimal with a comma (1,1). In Russian, words are written together: «ветерстих», not «ветер стих». The format hint is shown under the answer field.

If the format is right and the answer still isn’t accepted, send us a screenshot and we will fix it.

How do I reset my statistics?

Open the statistics of the subject and tap “Сбросить статистику по предмету” (Reset subject statistics). Other subjects are not affected.

I can’t hear the summary text

Audio plays like a media player: turn the volume up with the side buttons and check that no headphones or Bluetooth speaker are connected.

Will my progress move to a new iPhone?

The app has no account, so progress moves with your iPhone backup — via iCloud, a computer or Quick Start.

Do I need the internet?

No, the app works fully offline.

Is the app available in English?

No. The OGE is taken in Russian, so the app is in Russian too.

Privacy

The app collects no personal data and needs no account. Details are in the privacy policy.
